# 2. Program Structure

1. The Foundation program provides the basic knowledge about Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence (CSAI) through a set of core courses, which are compulsory for all students.
2. The rest of the program consists mostly of *elective courses*. An elective course is one that is not compulsory, and a student may choose from the courses he/she wants to take.
3. Besides electives for specialized areas, electives from domain areas (e.g. health, life sciences, finance, economics, E-Governance, sciences, etc.) may also be offered.
4. There will also be a set of Social Sciences and Humanities (SSH) courses offered.
5. A list of courses, and further information about the courses, is available on the website: https://www.iiitd.ac.in/academics/courses
6. A suggested schedule of courses is shown below (courses in the first two semesters must be followed as shown). Courses mentioned in [ ] or as a "bucket" are electives from a group of courses, and actual courses for these buckets are defined from semester to semester).

* AI Bucket: Any six courses (24 Credits of coursework) from the AI Bucket
  * IP/IS/BTP/OC, etc. are excluded.
  * A student must register with the AI course code to avail AI bucket credits. For dual code courses, a student must register the course with the AI course code to avail AI bucket credits.
  * The current list of courses is available at LINK

* *SSH Bucket: This bucket comprises any SSH course (SOC, PSY, ECO, SSH) with 1xx, 2xx, or 3xx course code, including those in the Ethics and Philosophy Bucket. The ENT courses are not part of this bucket. At least one course is mandatory from this bucket.

* **Ethics and Philosophy Bucket: This bucket comprises the following courses. At least one course is mandatory from this bucket.
  * SSH3PT Philosophy of Technology
  * SSH325/SSH525 Ethics in AI
  * SSH321/SSH521 Applied Ethics
  * SSH341/SSH541 Governing AI: History, Theory, Policy

* ***CSE Systems Bucket: This bucket comprises the following courses:
  * CSE202 Fundamentals of Database Management Systems
  * CSE232 Computer Networks
  * CSE511/ECE511 Computer Architecture
  At least one course is mandatory from this bucket.

* Open Electives: Any ten courses (40 Credits of coursework) from open electives >=3xx level across departments. An elective course is one that is not compulsory, and a student may choose from the courses he/she wants to take.
  * Includes IP/IS/UR/BTP/Online courses.
  * Only two 2xx level courses are allowed beyond the 4th semester.
