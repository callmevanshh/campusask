# 3. Requirements for Graduation

For a B.Tech. (CSD) degree, a student must satisfy all the following requirements:

1. Earn a total of 156 (inclusive of 2 credits each of SG/CW credits) credits (equivalent to 39 full courses of 4 credits).

2. Successfully complete all the core courses, and either Maths III or Maths IV or Discrete Mathematics or Theory of Computation.
