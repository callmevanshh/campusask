## overload

**Q:** my cgpa is 6.5, can I take 24 credits in one semester?

**A:** According to the UG Regulations (Oct 2025)...

* First-year students cannot take any overload [1, 13].
* Normal load for third- and fourth-year students is **16-22 credits**, with an overload of up to 2 additional credits (making it up to 24 credits) allowed only for students having a CGPA of more than 8.0 [1, 13].
* Second-year students having a CGPA of more than 8.0 can take an overload of up to 2 additional credits over 20 credits (making it up to 22 credits) [1, 13].
* If a student does not have a CGPA of more than 8.0, they may be allowed to take an overload only in one semester in the final year (either the 7th or 8th semester) [1].

**Application to your numbers:**
* Your CGPA is **6.5**, which is not greater than **8.0**. 
* Because your CGPA is **6.5**, which is less than or equal to **8.0**, you do not meet the CGPA condition for an overload unless you are in your final year (7th or 8th semester) and granted permission under that specific exception [1]. 
* 24 credits is greater than the normal load and exceeds the standard limits, constituting an overload [1, 13].

Please confirm your year of study, semester, and branch, and verify your eligibility with the academic office or your degree audit on the official portal.

**Problems:** none
