## 10. What happens if I fail 5 or more courses in the first year?

## 11. What restrictions apply to a student on academic warning?
Expected: answerable (gold pages [21])

Answer: A student placed on academic warning is subject to the following restrictions:
1. The student will not be allowed to hold any elected or nominated post for any institute body, including the hostel management, student activities, and student council [1].
2. The student will not be allowed to participate in any event outside the Institute as a member of the Institute team [1].
3. No academic overload is permitted [1, 2].
4. The DOAA may require the student to take an underload [1, 2].
5. The student must sign an undertaking agreeing to follow all stipulated conditions of the warning, which must be countersigned by the student's parent or guardian [2].

Cited pages: p.21, p.21

Grade: Correct
