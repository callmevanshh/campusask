## 7. Can I attend a refresher module to improve my F grade?
Expected: answerable (gold pages [16])

Answer: Yes, students with an F grade may attend refresher modules offered during vacations [1], [2]. ## 10. What happens if I fail 5 or more courses in the first year?
Expected: answerable (gold pages [20])

Answer: If a student fails 5 or more courses after completing the first year (1st and 2nd regular semesters, excluding summer), and did not take semester leave, they must repeat the first year [1]. 

For students repeating the first year:
*   The termination clause (7.3) will be applicable [1].
*   Credit transfers are only allowed for passed communication (COM101) and SSH courses from previous attempts [1].

Cited pages: p.20

Grade: Correct

## 11. What restrictions apply to a student on academic warning?
Expected: answerable (gold pages [21])

Answer: A student placed on academic warning is subject to the following restrictions:

*   They cannot hold any elected or nominated positions for any institute body, including hostel management, student activities, and the student council [1].
*   They are prohibited from participating in any events outside the Institute as a member of the Institute team [1].
*   No academic overload is permitted [1], [2].
*   The student may be required by the DOAA to take an underload [1], [2].
*   The student must sign an undertaking to follow all stipulated conditions of the warning, which must be countersigned by a parent or guardian [2].

Cited pages: p.21, p.21

Grade: Correct
