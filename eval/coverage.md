## What is the penalty for plagiarism or cheating in an exam?
**ANSWERED** | sources: Academic-Dishonesty.pdf p3

The provided documents specify the policy for plagiarism in assignments, but they do not explicitly state a separate penalty policy for cheating specifically during an exam [1]. 

Regarding plagiarism in assignments, the institute policy is as follows:
* **First instance:** One letter grade reduction [1].
* **Second instance:** F in the course [1].

The instructor decides the punishment and explains it to the student, who may present their case to the instructor or appeal the decision to the Disciplinary Action Committee (DAC) [1]. If the DAC finds the appeal frivolous, they may increase the punishment, including issuing an academic warning [1].

## What are the requirements for a minor?
**ANSWERED** | sources: UG-Regulations-2025-Oct.pdf p26

According to the UG Regulations (Oct 2025, section 8.4, page 26), the requirements for a minor are:
* A student enrolled in a B.Tech. program may take a minor in some other area [1].

## Can I get TA credits?
**ANSWERED** | sources: UG-Regulations-2025-Oct.pdf p7, UG-Regulations-2025-Oct.pdf p7

Yes, you can earn TA credits under the following rules:

* In a semester, a student can earn a maximum of 2 credits for TAship in a 4-credit course [2].
* UG students can take up to 4 credits as TA duty under the rules defined in the TA allocation policy [1].
* The grade for TAship can be S or X, which is the same as an online course [1, 2].
* Credits earned for doing a TAship will be counted within the approved bucket of 8 credits of online courses [1, 2].
* For the purpose of counting overload, the load of TAship will be considered equivalent to that of a 4-credit course [1].

* **Semester II (Winter Semester):** Starts after the first week of January and ends around the first week of May [5].
* **Summer Term:** Starts around the last week of May and ends around the last week of July [5].
